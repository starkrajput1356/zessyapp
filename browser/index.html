Frequently Asked Questions

What software and AI services does ZetsApp offer?

ZetsApp offers custom software development, AI and machine learning services (LLM apps, AI agents, RAG, chatbots), web and mobile app development, cloud and DevOps, system integrations, and a catalog of ready-to-use SaaS products you can adopt immediately.

How much does custom software development cost?

Cost depends on scope, complexity, and timeline. Most projects start with a free consultation and a fixed-scope quote. We offer flexible engagement models including fixed-price projects, dedicated teams, and monthly retainers.

Can you build AI solutions like chatbots and AI agents?

Yes. We design and ship AI products using leading models, including conversational chatbots, autonomous AI agents, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over your data, and custom ML models — deployed securely to production.

Do you have ready-made software products I can buy?

Yes. Alongside custom work, ZetsApp sells existing SaaS products across HR, CRM, finance, healthcare, and construction. Browse the software directory to compare features, pricing, and reviews, then contact us to get started.

How do you make sure the software ranks on Google?

Every page is built for SEO and GEO: server-friendly routing, fast Core Web Vitals, semantic HTML, canonical URLs, XML sitemaps, and rich structured data (Organization, SoftwareApplication, FAQ) so both Google and AI search engines can index and cite your pages.
