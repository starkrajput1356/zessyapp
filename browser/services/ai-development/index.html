Frequently asked questions

Which AI models do you work with?

We work with leading commercial and open-source models and choose the best fit for accuracy, cost, privacy, and latency for your use case. We are not tied to a single vendor.

Can you add AI to my existing software?

Yes. We integrate chatbots, copilots, and AI workflows directly into your current web or mobile applications via secure APIs, so you gain AI capabilities without a full rebuild.

How do you keep AI accurate and safe?

We ground models in your data with RAG, add evaluation harnesses and guardrails, and monitor outputs in production. Human-in-the-loop checkpoints handle decisions that need judgment.

Is my data safe with AI development?

Yes. We design for privacy — your data can stay within your own cloud or approved providers, with strict access controls over what any model can read or return. We can deploy open-source models on your infrastructure where required.

What is RAG and do I need it?

Retrieval-augmented generation grounds an LLM in your private, up-to-date knowledge so it answers from your content rather than guessing. If you need accurate answers over your own documents, policies, or product data, RAG is usually the right approach.
