Ask a question ↗
What software development services does ZetsApp offer?

ZetsApp offers custom software development, AI development and AI automation, web application development, mobile app development (iOS & Android), and a catalog of ready-to-use SaaS products. We cover the full lifecycle — discovery, UX design, architecture, development, QA, DevOps, and ongoing support.

How much do software development services cost?

Cost depends on scope, complexity, and timeline. Most projects start with a free consultation and a fixed-scope quote. We offer flexible engagement models including fixed-price projects, dedicated teams, staff augmentation, and monthly retainers.

How do you charge — fixed price or time and material?

Both. For well-defined projects we offer fixed-price, milestone-based engagements. For evolving products we recommend a dedicated team or time-and-material model so you can adapt scope as you learn.

Do I own the code and intellectual property?

Yes. You retain full ownership of all source code, designs, and intellectual property we produce for you, delivered in your own repositories.

How quickly can you start and deliver?

We can typically kick off within one to two weeks of scoping. A focused MVP usually ships in 6–12 weeks, with larger platforms delivered in phased releases so you see value early and often.

Can you work with our existing team and systems?

Absolutely. We integrate with your existing engineers, tools, and systems — CRMs, ERPs, data warehouses, and third-party APIs — through secure, well-documented interfaces.
