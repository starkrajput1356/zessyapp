About the Scientific & Graphing Calculator

A full scientific calculator in your browser: trigonometry (degrees/radians), logarithms, exponents, factorials, permutations and combinations, complex numbers, unit conversion and a history tape — plus a graphing mode for plotting functions, a matrix calculator and statistics (mean, median, standard deviation, regression).

Keyboard-friendly and free, it covers school, engineering and college maths without installing an app, and shows step-by-step evaluation for expressions.

How to use the scientific & graphing calculator

  1. 1Type or click an expression; use DEG/RAD for trigonometry.
  2. 2Press = to evaluate; results and history are kept for reuse.
  3. 3Switch to Graph mode to plot y = f(x) and inspect intercepts.
  4. 4Use Matrix and Statistics modes for determinants, inverses, mean and standard deviation.

Scientific & Graphing Calculator — frequently asked questions

Does it work in degrees and radians?

Yes — toggle DEG/RAD; the mode applies to sin, cos, tan and their inverses.

Can I plot a function?

Enter an expression in x (e.g. x^2 − 3x + 2) in Graph mode to plot it, zoom, and read intercepts and turning points.

Does it handle matrices?

Yes — add up to 4×4 matrices for determinant, inverse, transpose, addition and multiplication.

Is it accurate for exams?

It uses IEEE double precision (about 15 significant digits) like handheld scientific calculators; check your exam board's rules on online tools.

Can I use my keyboard?

Yes — digits, operators, parentheses, Enter for equals and Backspace to delete.
