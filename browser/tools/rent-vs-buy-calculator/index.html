Rent vs Buy Calculator 2026 — Complete Financial Guide

Our rent vs buy calculator provides a comprehensive financial comparison using real-world cost assumptions. Unlike basic calculators, we model the full cost of homeownership including property taxes, maintenance (1-2% annually), insurance, HOA fees, and closing costs — and compare it against renting while investing the difference in a diversified portfolio.

Key factors in your rent vs buy decision: the price-to-rent ratio in your market, how long you plan to stay, current mortgage rates, your down payment saved, and your expected home appreciation rate. Our 2026 rates reflect current market conditions for accurate projections.
