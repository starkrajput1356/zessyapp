About the Stamp Duty & Property Tax Calculator

Calculate stamp duty, registration charges and property transfer taxes for a home purchase in India (state-wise rates for Maharashtra, Karnataka, Delhi, Tamil Nadu and more), the UK (SDLT bands incl. first-time-buyer and additional-property surcharges), Australia, Canada and US transfer taxes.

Enter the property value, location and buyer type to get the duty, registration fee and total upfront cost — the amounts most buyers underestimate when budgeting the down payment.

Stamp Duty & Property Tax Calculator — frequently asked questions

How much is stamp duty in India?

Typically 5–7% of the property value plus ~1% registration, varying by state: e.g. Maharashtra 5% (+1% metro cess), Karnataka 5%, Delhi 6% (4% for women). The calculator applies the current state schedule.

How is UK stamp duty (SDLT) calculated?

In bands: 0% up to the threshold, then rising rates on each slice above it; first-time buyers get a higher nil-rate band and additional properties pay a surcharge. The calculator computes the banded total automatically.

Is stamp duty charged on the agreement value or circle rate?

On the higher of the two in most Indian states — the agreement value or the government ready-reckoner/circle rate.

Can stamp duty be claimed as a tax deduction?

In India, stamp duty and registration on a home can be claimed under Section 80C (within the ₹1.5 lakh limit) in the year of payment.

Do women get a stamp duty concession?

Several Indian states (Delhi, Haryana, UP, Maharashtra in some cases) charge 1–2% less when the property is registered in a woman's name — select "female owner" to apply it.
